Voluntad Popular thanks and acknowledges the investigation by Cazadores de Fake News, whose alert allowed us to identify a group of at least 8 activists and 2 institutional municipal and parish accounts that collaborated with a network that for years had been attacking Leopoldo López and Juan Guaidó and that recently drove an amplification campaign with inauthentic accounts in favor of Alejandro Betancourt and his company NABEP, linked to the oil agreement between Venezuela and the United States.

Following the questions raised by Cazadores Fakenews, the National Leadership of Voluntad Popular opened and conducted an internal investigation that identified Alexander Tirado as one of those chiefly responsible. By then, he had already been removed from his duties as National Activism Coordinator as a result of another disciplinary process, over a different and serious case that is under investigation before the competent authorities. Despite this, he used his contacts and internal relationships to drive this campaign and to involve party structures and activists in private interests unrelated to the party's goals.

The investigation is examining possible acts of infiltration, personal and monetary interests, as well as the manipulation of activists and the exploitation of their economic needs. We are alarmed that people who enjoyed the trust of our organization collaborated with a network used to attack Leopoldo López, Juan Guaidó and other opposition leaders and to defend the image of people who have been part of major corruption cases against the country.

As a result of the checks carried out, Tirado and other activists involved were expelled from the party, and removals from posts and from responsibilities were applied. The investigation continues: we will go all the way to identify those who may have acted as infiltrators or involved the party in these events, clarify all responsibilities and sanction everyone responsible.

As stated in the Cazadores FakeNews report, Voluntad Popular did not organize, hire or authorize these operations, nor did it give instructions to support Alejandro Betancourt or his businesses. We categorically reject these shameful and intolerable practices, which damage the digital ecosystem, manipulate public opinion and undermine citizens' trust.

We firmly oppose corrupt figures such as Alejandro Betancourt, who have plundered the Venezuelan State, and we will not allow our structures to be used to defend them or to promote their image and their interests. We will not tolerate these practices under any circumstances. We will have zero tolerance for the use of the party's name or accounts for personal or commercial interests, or for interests unrelated to our organization.

We call on democratic parties and organizations to remain alert to possible acts of infiltration and manipulation, as well as to the use of their structures for private interests.

This investigation reaffirms the importance of independent journalism and freedom of expression in detecting and exposing these events. Our commitment is to the Venezuelan people, freedom and democracy.

Voluntad Popular  
October 6, 2026
