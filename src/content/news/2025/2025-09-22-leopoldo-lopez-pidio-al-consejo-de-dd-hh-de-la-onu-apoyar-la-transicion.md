The national head of Voluntad Popular (VP), Leopoldo López, **urged the United Nations Human Rights Council on Monday to support the transition to democracy in Venezuela and the fight against the Cartel de los Soles**, following the publication of the new report of the Independent International Fact-Finding Mission, which documents serious crimes committed by Nicolás Maduro’s regime.

“This report confirms what we Venezuelans know all too well. **Nicolás Maduro stays in power through systematic repression, torture and electoral fraud,” López said in his speech.**

He asserted that **Venezuela cannot be considered “an imperfect democracy,” but rather “an apparatus of power that has destroyed the rule of law,** has collapsed the economy, has generated a complex humanitarian crisis and has displaced a third of the population.”

According to the VP leader, the Mission’s findings document **“enforced disappearances, torture, sexual violence against detainees and the imprisonment of children on terrorism charges.”**

In this context, he recalled that his own history reflects that persecution: **“Ten years ago I was sentenced to 14 years in prison for denouncing Maduro as a narco, a repressor and corrupt.** The same accusations that this report confirms today.”

López stressed that the Venezuelan crisis has a political and a criminal component. **He recalled that in 2020 the United Nations recognized the existence of the Cartel de los Soles and maintained that “today we know that it is not a parallel group, but the State** transformed into a cartel that traffics drugs, launders money and exports violence.”

The leader also referred to the result of the recent elections: **“On July 28, Venezuela elected Edmundo González. However, Maduro struck a blow against popular sovereignty and remains in power by force,** with the backing of Russia, China, Iran and other autocratic countries.”

Faced with this situation, López called for firm action by the UN Human Rights Council: “That is why I urge this Council to **recognize the will of the Venezuelan people, support the democratic transition, demand justice and back the international coalition against the Cártel de los Soles.”**
