The Voluntad Popular (VP) party and its national head, Leopoldo Lopez, congratulated María Corina Machado on Thursday after she was awarded the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize, **a recognition they described as a tribute “to the struggle for the freedom of Venezuela.”**

**“Today, the world recognizes what we Venezuelans have so often witnessed: that the struggle for the freedom of Venezuela is a struggle for PEACE,”** VP wrote on its X account.

The party highlighted that the prize was awarded to the opposition leader **“for her tireless work promoting democratic rights for the people of Venezuela** and for her struggle to achieve a just and peaceful transition from dictatorship to democracy.”

In its message, **Voluntad Popular stated that this prize also belongs to the Venezuelan people “who have resisted with courage, dignity and faith.”**

“The first Nobel Peace Prize awarded to a Venezuelan heralds the dawn of a new era: **the victory of good over evil, of truth over lies, of decency over corruption, of democracy over dictatorship,”** the organization added.

VP stated that the recognition **represents “the confirmation of a greater certainty that resides in the heart of every Venezuelan: Venezuela will be free!”**

## **Leopoldo López: “A recognition of courage”**

For his part, the party’s national head, **Leopoldo López, also expressed his congratulations to Machado. “Congratulations @MariaCorinaYA on this well-deserved recognition of your courage** and tireless struggle for democracy, freedom and human rights,” he wrote on X.

**López added that the Nobel “also honors the strength of a people determined to bring about change,** whom nothing and no one will stop until they achieve a free and democratic Venezuela.”
