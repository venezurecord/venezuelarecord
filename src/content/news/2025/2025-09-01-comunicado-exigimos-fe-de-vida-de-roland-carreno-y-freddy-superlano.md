At Voluntad Popular, we denounce with deep concern that our brothers in the struggle, Roland Carreño and Freddy Superlano, **political prisoners of the Venezuelan narco-state, have been moved to an unknown location.**

**It became known unofficially that both were taken out of the Helicoide more than 24 hours ago,** dressed in blue and yellow coveralls, and transported in SEBIN vehicles. **Since then, their destination is unknown.** At one point there was speculation that they might have been brought before the courts, but there is no official information about their current location.

**We warn that this pattern of enforced disappearances is a criminal method of Nicolás Maduro’s regime to increase political persecution and psychological torture against our leaders and their families.**

**We immediately demand that the regime provide proof of life for Roland Carreño and Freddy Superlano;** their immediate release, since they never should have been imprisoned; and an end to the policy of hatred and persecution against those of us who fight for a free Venezuela.

We make an urgent call to the international community, multilateral bodies and human rights defenders **to speak out firmly against this serious violation, which endangers the lives and integrity of our brothers.**

**Enough persecution!**
